Porter lets a sender lock a scheduled USDC payout plus a caller bounty, then lets anyone settle the room once due.
It uses Arc so payout, bounty, and gas share USDC, allowing callers to assess the fee without holding a second gas token.
The [opening transaction](https://explorer.arc.io/tx/0x18ab61c5dd1c812a7bdd1096ad096e6d9bfd48b1db9bc86329db331cc2353835) and [settlement transaction](https://explorer.arc.io/tx/0x1e90ded621847c72c7ae6a45f87f6b5c73cbc4928609fde654df27071e5fb2a1) prove 0.1 USDC reached the payee and 0.01 USDC reached the caller.
It is an early builder-controlled payment prototype, not a keeper network, a bounty-over-gas guarantee, or evidence of independent use.
